# P06-T08 - Complete MVP Acceptance

- Type: Task
- Phase: 06 - Operations and Release
- Parent: P06-S01
- Depends on: P06-T07
- Pair: Senior Developer + Senior QA
- Status: Backlog

## Outcome

The Product Owner has enough operational and quality evidence to accept or reject the MVP.

## Work

- Complete the operator runbook for connection, permission, stale-plan, worker, rate-limit, partial-failure, restore, and cancellation events.
- Map product acceptance criteria to system evidence and record exceptions with owner and due date.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Every MVP criterion links to passing evidence or an explicit Product Owner exception.
- [ ] A second operator follows the runbook for one simulated failure without author assistance.
- [ ] Release version, known limitations, rollback boundary, and support owner are recorded.

## Evidence

- [ ] Signed MVP acceptance record and reviewed operator exercise report.
